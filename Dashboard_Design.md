# Power BI Dashboard Design

## Page 1 — Executive Market Overview
KPI cards:
- Total Oil Production
- Total Oil Consumption
- Total Gas Production
- Total Gas Consumption
- Average Oil Price
- Average Gas Price

Visuals:
- Line chart: Oil production vs consumption by year
- Bar chart: Top 10 oil-producing countries
- Map: Oil production by country
- Donut/bar chart: Production by region

## Page 2 — Country Market Intelligence
Slicers:
- Country
- Region
- Year

Visuals:
- KPI cards for production, consumption, imports and exports
- Production vs consumption trend
- Supply-demand gap
- Import dependency
- Oil/gas price trend

## Page 3 — Supply & Demand Analysis
Visuals:
- Country ranking by supply-demand gap
- Import dependency by country
- Supply surplus vs supply deficit classification
- Regional comparison
- Year trend

## Page 4 — Commodity Price Intelligence
Visuals:
- Oil price trend
- Gas price trend
- Production vs price comparison
- Country/region slicers

## Interview story
The dashboard is designed to help an energy-market analyst identify:
1. Major production markets
2. Persistent supply deficits
3. Import-dependent markets
4. Demand-growth markets
5. Commodity-price movements
6. Potential market opportunities
